Copied! Now follow the steps below.

iPhone Mail

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature, delete the old one and paste.
  3. Right after pasting, shake your phone and tap Undo Change Attributes.
  4. Go back to save, then send a test email.

Apple Mail / Outlook on a computer

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Apple Mail: Settings → Signatures → your @advio.com account → + → paste, and uncheck "Always match my default message font".
  3. Outlook: Settings → Signatures → New → paste, and set it as the default.
